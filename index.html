About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Henan University, China, specialising in cybersecurity and trustworthy AI for cyber-physical and distributed systems. My research focuses on securing critical infrastructures — smart grids, industrial IoT, and medical systems — through a combination of rigorous security protocol design, privacy-preserving machine learning, and adversarially robust AI.

I have published 27 research papers (9 as first author) in leading Q1 venues including IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, and IEEE Internet of Things Journal, accumulating over 306 citations (h-index: 10).

My current research directions include LLM-driven malware detection, zero-day attack analysis, AI-based vulnerability discovery, and federated learning under realistic threat models. I am the Principal Investigator of a Henan Province Postdoctoral Research grant (CNY 100,000) focused on privacy-preserving analytics for smart-grid environments.

Research Areas

My research spans six interconnected domains at the frontier of cybersecurity and AI.

🔐

Cybersecurity Protocols & Formal Security

Authentication, access control, key agreement, PUF-based schemes, formal verification (ProVerif, AVISPA), and privacy-by-design architectures for smart grids and IoT.

🤖

Trustworthy & Robust AI

Adversarial robustness against poisoning and evasion attacks, intrusion detection systems, secure ML pipelines, and degradation-aware evaluation under realistic threat models.

🦠

Malware Analysis & Threat Intelligence

Zero-day attack pattern analysis, AI/LLM-driven vulnerability discovery, exploit generation analysis, and behaviour-based detection of advanced and unseen threats.

🌐

Federated & Privacy-Preserving ML

Secure aggregation, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption (SEAL, HElib), and Byzantine-robust aggregation for distributed learning across IoT and medical environments.

⚡

Efficient Transformer Systems & Edge AI

Lightweight transformer backbones, FLOPs/latency-aware model optimisation, and deployment-ready AI for IIoT, UAV, and cyber-physical systems.

💡

LLMs & Code Intelligence

Representation learning for code and binary artefacts, semantics-preserving transformations, and LLM-driven security-relevant software analysis.
