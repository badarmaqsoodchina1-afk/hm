## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow specialising in **cybersecurity** and **trustworthy AI** for cyber-physical and distributed systems. My work focuses on securing critical infrastructures — from smart grids and IoT to medical and industrial environments — through a combination of formal security protocols, privacy-preserving machine learning, and adversarially robust AI.

My current research directions include **LLM-driven malware detection**, **zero-day attack analysis**, **AI-based vulnerability discovery**, and **federated learning** under realistic threat models. I bridge theoretical advances in cybersecurity with deployment-ready solutions.

---

## Research Areas

| Area | Topics |
|---|---|
| **Cybersecurity Protocols & Formal Security** | Authentication, access control, key agreement, PUF-based schemes, formal verification (ProVerif, AVISPA), privacy-by-design for smart grids & IoT |
| **Trustworthy & Robust AI** | Adversarial robustness (poisoning/evasion), intrusion detection, secure ML pipelines, degradation-aware evaluation |
| **Malware Analysis & Threat Intelligence** | Zero-day attack patterns, AI/LLM-driven vulnerability discovery, exploit analysis, behaviour-based threat detection |
| **Federated & Privacy-Preserving ML** | Secure aggregation, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, Byzantine-robust aggregation for distributed environments |
| **Efficient Transformer Systems & Edge AI** | Lightweight transformer backbones, FLOPs-aware optimisation, deployment-ready AI for IIoT and cyber-physical systems |
| **LLMs & Code Intelligence** | Representation learning for code/binaries, semantics-preserving transformations, LLM-driven security analysis |
